# Faculty Handbook (Staff Only)

This handbook is for teaching and administrative staff. It is not part of the
student-facing knowledge base: the retrieval layer tags every document under
`data/raw/staff/` as staff-visible, so a student session can never retrieve or
be answered from it.

## 1. Attendance appeals

A student may appeal an attendance shortfall within ten working days of the
result being published. The appeal must state the dates in dispute and attach
supporting evidence (medical certificate, official duty letter, or event
participation proof).

The department attendance committee meets on the first and third Thursday of
each month. A quorum is three staff members. The committee may condone up to
15 % of the required attendance; anything above that requires the Dean's
approval.

## 2. Mark review procedure

A mark review is not a re-evaluation. The reviewing staff member checks three
things only: totals were added correctly, every answered question was marked,
and the published grade matches the marksheet.

Reviews are accepted for fourteen days after results are published. The
reviewer records the outcome in the internal marksheet and notifies the
student through the department office, never by email from a personal address.

## 3. Internal moderation

Every end-semester paper is moderated by a second staff member before results
are released. The moderator signs the moderation sheet, and the sheet is filed
with the examination cell. Moderation comments are internal: they are never
shared with students and are not part of the answer system.

## 4. Escalation contacts

Attendance disputes escalate from the class advisor to the department head to
the Dean of Academics. Placement disputes escalate to the Training and
Placement Officer. A staff member who is unsure which route applies should
contact the department office rather than the student's family.

## 5. Data handling

Staff may view student records only for students in their assigned department.
A query that ranges over a cohort is logged with the staff identity, the
department and the number of rows returned. Exporting a student's eligibility
report is logged as a personal-data read.
